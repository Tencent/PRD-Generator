# Code of Conduct

(中文版见下方。)

We are committed to a friendly, safe and inclusive community for everyone, regardless of experience level, gender, age, ethnicity, nationality, religion or any other personal characteristic.

## Expected behaviour

- Respect different views and experiences, and communicate kindly and patiently;
- Address the issue, not the person; make criticism specific and constructive;
- Give and accept constructive feedback, and own your mistakes.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, insulting or discriminatory remarks, harassment;
- Publishing other people's private information without permission;
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits and issues that violate this code, and may restrict participation of repeat offenders. If you encounter a violation, use GitHub's reporting feature or contact a maintainer privately.

# 行为准则

我们致力于为所有参与者提供友好、安全、包容的社区环境，无论其经验水平、性别、年龄、种族、国籍、宗教信仰或其他个人特征。

## 期望的行为

- 尊重不同的观点与经验，友善、耐心地沟通；
- 对事不对人，批评要具体、有建设性；
- 接受并给出建设性的反馈，对错误坦然承担。

## 不可接受的行为

- 人身攻击、侮辱性或歧视性言论、骚扰；
- 未经许可公开他人的隐私信息；
- 其他在专业场合中不恰当的行为。

## 执行

项目维护者有权删除、编辑或拒绝违反本准则的评论、提交与 Issue，并可对反复违规者采取限制参与的措施。如遇违规行为，请通过 GitHub 的举报功能或私下联系维护者。
